SCTC Round Timer

Privacy Policy and Support

Effective November 25, 2026

Data collection and use

SCTC Round Timer is provided by Halter Technical Limited Liability Company. The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. There are no user accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services. All timer functionality operates on the device.

Local preferences

Your selected preset and custom timer settings are saved locally on your device so they remain available when you reopen the app. System timing information is used only to run the countdown. Neither preferences nor timing information is sent to us. You can remove locally stored preferences by deleting the app. No account or server records need to be deleted.

Audio

The app plays bundled sound cues and may briefly lower the volume of other audio playing on the device. It does not record audio or access your music library.

Support and contact

For help with the app, privacy questions, or feedback, contact Halter Technical. Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support is used to respond to your request. We do not collect such information through the app itself.
